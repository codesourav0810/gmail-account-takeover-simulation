Safety Measures

Simple security practices can prevent account takeovers, phishing attacks, and unauthorized access.

Enable Two-Factor Authentication

Add an extra layer of security to your account using two-factor authentication.

Verify Links Before Clicking

Check the website address carefully before entering your password or personal information.

Never Share Your Password

Keep your passwords private and never share them through messages, calls, or emails.

Never Share OTPs

A legitimate service will not ask you to disclose an OTP received on your device.

Be Careful With Suspicious Emails

Do not trust urgent messages asking you to verify, unlock, or secure your account immediately.

Review Active Devices

Regularly check your account activity and remove devices or sessions you do not recognize.

ABOUT THE PROJECT

Understanding the Attack

This project is an interactive educational simulation that demonstrates how a Gmail account takeover scam can trick a user into revealing sensitive information.

The simulation presents both sides of the attack — the victim's screen and the attacker's screen — showing how information can be captured and misused during a phishing attempt.

The purpose of this project is to create awareness and help users recognize common warning signs such as suspicious links, fake verification pages, and OTP requests.
